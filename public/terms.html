Terms

Last updated 2026-11-24. Plain-language terms for a personal app. They are not legal advice.

Who can use it

LiveArchive is invite only. You may use it for your own record of the events you attend. Signing in without an invitation shows "not invited" and gives no access.

Your data

The events, people and notes you add are yours. You can export them as CSV and delete them. See the Privacy page for what is stored and where.

Lineups and sources

Lineups, dates and set times come from third-party sources. The app shows where each act came from and leaves out anything it cannot find. It can still be wrong or out of date, so check the source before you rely on it. The app does not sell tickets.

No warranty

The app is provided as it is, without a warranty of any kind. Its owner is not liable for lost data or decisions made from what it shows. Keep your own backup, such as a CSV export, if the record matters to you.

Leaving

You can stop using the app at any time. You can delete your data in Your account, or ask the person who invited you to remove it.

Changes

These terms can change. When they do, the date at the top changes too.
