Privacy

Last updated 2026-11-24. This describes what the code does today. It is not legal advice.

What LiveArchive stores

For each event you add: its name, dates, venue, city, the acts you saw, ratings, prices, and any notes. Companions (the people you went with) and notes can be encrypted in your browser with a passphrase you choose. When that vault is on, the server holds only the encrypted text and the salts. It never holds your passphrase or the key.

Events in Recently deleted are erased after 90 days. Activity log rows are erased after a year.

Signing in

Access to the app is by invitation only. Sign-in goes through Cloudflare Access, which checks your Google account. The app also checks Cloudflare's sign-in token on each request. If your Google account has no tracker here, you can ask for access. The owner sees the email on your Google account and decides. The app keeps that email, and the date you asked and the owner's decisions, so that a declined email stays declined. When you are invited or approved, the app may send an email to that address with sign-in instructions or a welcome note; you can opt out in one click from the link in any email or in Your account. To ask for your record to be removed, contact the owner of this app directly.

Google data

Check email reads your Gmail, read-only, and only messages from the ticket senders the app knows how to read. It reads the message contents on the server to find shows, and it keeps the refresh token that lets it check again. What it finds is put on a review list, and nothing is saved until you tick it.

The browser email scan signs you in with Google in your browser for that one scan. The app does not keep that sign-in.

Google Sheets sync writes your log to a sheet you choose. The sign-in token is stored sealed with an encryption key that is held only as a secret of the server. The people who run the server can still use that token, so treat the sheet and the server as trusted.

The app's use of Google data follows Google's API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

What other services receive

  • Lookup sources (Clashfinder, promoter pages, Ticketmaster, MusicBrainz, setlist.fm, Brave Search) receive the names, dates and places of the events you look up. Open-Meteo receives the city and dates of an event when its weather is looked up.
  • Gemini (Google's AI model) reads only web pages the app fetched: lineup pages when you find a lineup, the pages found for an event when you press "Deep search with AI", and, after you save an event whose date, venue or city is still missing, up to 2 of those pages on its own. It never receives your email text or your personal notes.
  • Email delivery: when account emails are turned on, Resend receives the recipient email address and the invite, approval or request-digest message to deliver it.
  • Hosting and database: the app runs on Cloudflare Workers and stores its data in Supabase.

Sharing

A share link you create shows the events and figures you choose to include, to anyone with the link. Turn it off to stop the page from being served. Share pages are not indexed by search engines.

Your data

You can download your events as a CSV file from the Table view. Deleting an event moves it to Recently deleted; it is erased after 90 days. You can download or delete all your data in Your account. To ask for anything else, contact the person who invited you.

Changes

When this page changes, its date at the top changes too.
