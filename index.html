LiveArchive

Remember every show you went to and everyone you saw there.

Type one line about a concert or festival. LiveArchive finds the date, venue and lineup from the sources it reads, shows where each name came from, and keeps your record of who you saw.

See the demo Log in (invite only)

How it works

1. Type one line

"Portola 2025" or "Coachella, April". Text the app cannot place stays in the name, so nothing is guessed.

2. Read the lineup

Lineups come from the sources that publish them, such as festival timetables, promoter pages and Ticketmaster. Each act links back to where it was read.

3. Tick who you saw

Nothing joins your record until you tick it. Set times, stages and back-to-back sets are kept only where a source gives them.

What you get

Summary and Table

Every event as a row, with filters, sorting and CSV export.

Timeline

Your shows laid out by month, year and season.

Map

A dot for each city you went to, with year playback.

Stats and Ranking

Events per year, streaks, your most-seen acts and series you went back to.

Share

A read-only page of your history that you can send to a friend.

Google Sheets

An optional one-way copy of your log into a sheet you choose.

Honest by design

It never guesses a lineup

A date, venue, act or set time is stored only when a source gives it. Missing data stays missing, and the app says so.

Every act has a source

Each name links to the page it came from, with the exact words that named it.

Ticket emails stay out of AI

Ticket emails are read by code written for each seller. An AI model never reads them.

Lineup sources are credited on each event. LiveArchive does not sell tickets.

Your privacy

Your log is yours. Companions and notes can be encrypted in your browser, so the server never holds the key. The privacy page says exactly what is stored, what Google data is read, and what other services receive.

Invite only for now

LiveArchive is invite only. Sign-in goes through Google, and an account that is not on the invite list is shown "not invited" and gets no access. There is no sign-up form. Read the terms before you use it.
