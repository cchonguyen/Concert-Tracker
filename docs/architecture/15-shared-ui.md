# Shared UI

Part of [ARCHITECTURE.md](../../ARCHITECTURE.md).

A copyable export of these tokens, components and the voice rules, for starting a new app, is in [docs/design-system/](../design-system/README.md). Update it when a shared component or token changes.


Reusable controls live in `src/components/ui/`: `IconButton` (an icon with a required label, shown as a title and aria-label, and as a short tooltip after a tap on touch), `FilterControls` (the Filters button, its panel, pills and yes/no toggle, shared by the Table, Map and timeline pages), `BackToTop` (a text pill, "Back to top", that appears after about one screen of scroll, bottom right; on a phone it sits above the + button), `MenuButton` (shows the current choice and opens a list; closes on pick, outside tap or Escape), `Popover` (an anchored panel that opens on hover with a mouse and on tap with touch, and closes on an outside tap or Escape) and `Pill` (a small rounded label). The rule for new work is in AGENTS.md ("Design system"). Motion follows `docs/specs/motion-whimsy.md`: every control fades its colour changes and squashes a little when pressed, `IconButton` icons tip on hover, tab marks and the `Segmented` fill slide with a small overshoot, `Popover` and `MenuButton` panels pop in, and all of it stops under reduced motion. Only the components the current change needs are in this folder, and more move in as the pages they belong to are touched.

Field saves in the event panel wait 500 ms after the last key (`Field` in `EventDetail.tsx`) and save at once on leaving the field, so a closing sheet still keeps the last value. Every save from the panel is tracked by `SaveStatus` (`src/components/ui/SaveStatus.tsx`), whose line reads "Saving…", "Saved · just now" (aging to "2 min ago"), or "Couldn't save · Try again". The state is a pure machine in `src/services/saveStatus.ts`: a failed patch is kept until Try again resends it and that save works. A saved field flashes a green edge for about a second (no motion when reduced motion is set). Closing blurs the field being typed in, then waits for any save in flight; if one failed, the panel stays open. "Changes save as you go" shows on the first three opens of an event on a device (`liveArchive.saveHint` in `localStorage`, read in try/catch). Icon buttons are 44 px square on phones and 36 px from `sm` up (`IconButton`), and the dismiss buttons on tips and callouts are 40 px. The event detail is one surface (`DetailShell`, `src/components/ui/DetailShell.tsx`) in three widths: a split view from 1100 px, with the list of events on the left (240 px, or 280 px from 1440 px); a slide-over from 768 px, 92% of the width, over the page dimmed in the strip that is left; and a bottom sheet below 768 px, 92% of the height, with a drag handle that closes it when dragged down from the top of its content. Its header holds the save status on the left, then "N of M in your sort", previous and next (`ChevronUp` and `ChevronDown`), "Next with gaps" and close, all bordered; the split list has a header with the event count and a button back to the full list, and the open row is tinted with a violet edge; J and K (or the arrow keys, unless a choice group has focus) move to the previous and next event, N to the next event below with a gap, and Escape closes; keys are ignored while a text field has focus. Opening an event pushes `?event=<id>` onto the address (`openEvent` and `closeEvent` in `App.tsx`, read with `eventParam` and `withEventParam` in `src/services/detailNav.ts`), so Back closes it and a link reopens it; moving to another event replaces that entry. The list is the view the event was opened from: the Table opens its current filters and sort (`filterEvents` and `sortRows`), and every other view opens newest first by date (`sortRows` on date). Its order is read live from the current log while the detail is open. Gap badges count the kinds `hasGap` finds missing. Moving to another event waits for saves in flight, as closing does. The detail does not blur its backdrop, and the list does not preload neighbouring events yet. The top of the detail is a poster (`PosterHeader` in `src/components/PosterHeader.tsx`), edge to edge above the padded content (the shell's `hero` slot): a line with the dates, venue and city, your rating as five stars in its corner (tapping one rates; tapping the current rating clears it), the name, and the bill from `billLines` in `src/services/poster.ts`. Headliners you saw are the large line; with none billed as one, the seen acts with no role are. Other seen acts are the medium line, and cited lineup acts you did not see are counted as "+ N more". Sizes never come from list order. Status pills show Counted, Upcoming, Did you go? or Didn't go, then "Date confirmed · N sources" when sources confirm the date and "Lineup · N acts, all cited" when there is a lineup. Under the poster, the things-left strip lists the gaps `hasGap` finds for this event (who you saw, dates and favourite set first, then the rest in table order), or reads "All filled in" when nothing is missing. The strip has a chip per gap, the question for the gap being asked about (`GAP_QUESTION`) and a Next button that moves to the next gap; a chip or Next goes to the section that fills that gap (`GAP_SECTION` in `poster.ts`; the sections have the ids `when-where`, `who-you-saw` and `your-notes`) and highlights it for a moment. There is no separate guided card. Check details lives in "Where every detail came from" (`EventSources`), which is always the last card, full width below both columns (so below Who you saw on phones too): Check details and, after a check, "Deep search with AI" sit side by side beside the heading, both showing the music bars while they run; what they do and the AI reads left today are in an info tooltip beside the heading (`help`), not under the buttons. What the check finds is listed under it (`CheckDetails` with `inline`): once the card is at least 760 px wide the sources it asks (Ticketmaster, setlist.fm, Clashfinder) sit side by side, and each source row spreads across three columns (the source, what it gave with its quote, and its link); narrower, the source and link share a line with what it gave below. Read rows (`ReadRow`) hold one field each in shared columns, so icons, labels, values and the right-hand icons line up: the row icon, the label, the value, an optional action, and (on phones) a right slot. A row takes the shared hover tint (`row-hover` in `index.css`, `docs/specs/row-hover.md`): it comes in at once and fades out over 0.8 seconds, only where a pointer can hover, with no fade under reduced motion; the same class tints Ledger rows, the split view list, lineup rows, act rows and the artist and river lists. Where a value came from, inferred values (with Confirm or Verify) and notes such as "Shows as …" open from one "i" icon: from `sm` up it sits beside the label, no column is kept for the edit icon, and the value runs to the right edge; on hover the edit icon lays a square over the row's right edge, as its own click target, on that row only (`group/row`). On phones a tap on the row edits it, so the info icon takes the right slot. `Popover` waits 200 ms before a hover closes it and has no gap between trigger and panel, so a sweeping pointer does not drop it. An inferred value shows in the row in italics. The Date row lists each day on its own line with a checkbox (ticked unless missed, even for a one-day event, once you went or said you didn't); unticking the only day left asks "Did you not go to this event?" and marks it missed. The row carries every day, so there is no separate end-date row: its editor holds the date and the last day, and its "+" sits where the edit icon would be, always shown, with a wide hover area, and offers "Edit dates" (opens the date and last-day fields) and, for a series, "Edit weekends" (opens the Weekend row). Weekend shows only when the edition has several weekends, one is picked, or it is opened from the "+". Type is a two-part control (`Segmented`), Festival or Concert. A magnifying glass at the top right of The edition runs the plain Check details (never Deep search), showing the music bars (`IconButton` with `busy`) while it runs; "Where every detail came from" shows what it finds. The Weather row's own glass sits where the edit icon would be and looks up the weather with the same bars; it shows only once the event's last day has passed and it has a city. Who you saw lists each act as a row: the heart toggles the favourite set (one per event), and the rest of the row opens the act's editor, showing DJ or Live as quiet text, Headliner, Solo, B2B partners joined by a quiet "B2B", and the set time on the right. A missing set type is not flagged there; it is set in the act's editor or in Edit lineup. "When and where" and "Who you saw" have a "?" popover (`SectionHelp`). "Your night" is a `details` element whose open state is kept per device in `localStorage` (`src/services/sectionMemory.ts`, read and written in try/catch). "Yes, I went" and turning on the favourite set burst a few marks, once, unless reduced motion is set (`Burst`, `src/components/ui/Burst.tsx`). In split view the detail is the Table's width (`max-w-6xl`), so it reads as part of the same page. The body is two columns once its own width is at least 760 px (a container query on the shell's content, `@[760px]:`): the left column holds Name and series (the Weekend row shows only when the edition has several weekends or one is stored, or when opened from the Date row's "+"), Did you go? when it is due, and When and where; the right holds Who you saw, Full lineup (a card with its find and edit buttons) and Your night (the `your-notes` section, whose title shows who you went with and the ticket price when you've given them, and whose "Edit on every event" link sits at the bottom). Upcoming events say on the poster that they are not counted yet; the "Did you go?" card is shown only once an event is over. Type is two chips, Festival and Concert: until you pick one the worked-out type is lit with a ✦ marker, and picking a chip stores it. Narrower, it is one column. The poster uses the design's two glows over the panel, and its bill sizes come from billing.

### Design refresh 2.0 parts

The design refresh (`docs/specs/design-refresh-2.md`) adds plain-named colour tokens in `src/index.css` (`bg-surface-1`, `text-text-muted`, `border-line`, `bg-accent-fill text-on-accent`, `bg-accent-tint`, `text-good`, `text-warn`, `text-bad`, and the `font-display` and `font-mono` roles). `tests/themeContrast.test.ts` checks them at 4.5:1 in all 10 themes. Shared parts built for it:

- `Tabs` (`src/components/ui/Tabs.tsx`) handles navigation between pages or views. It has two looks:
  - `folder`: square tabs on one bottom line. The open tab joins the panel below it, with a raised surface, a 2 px accent cap, bold text, an accent icon and an optional mono count pill.
  - `underline`: a quieter row with a 2 px straight underline, used for page rows.

  The cap or underline slides between tabs with `motion` and moves at once under reduced motion. The open tab is marked with `aria-current="page"` and never uses the solid Add colour. With `compact`, phones show only the icon for tabs that are not open.

- `Ledger` (`src/components/ui/Ledger.tsx`): joined, square-cornered rows. `Ledger.Row` carries a 3 px straight edge bar on the left for the kind: `festival` (accent), `show` (grey) or `needs` (amber), always paired with a `KindPill` or `HintPill` in the row. Hover lifts the row one shade and widens the bar to 4 px, with no motion. `Ledger.Separator` is a year separator ("2026 · 5 events", or "No date yet"). Lists use year separators, never month headers.
- `KindPill` (`src/components/ui/KindPill.tsx`): Festival, Show or "Type not known", each with an icon. The app never guesses a kind.
- `HintPill` (`src/components/ui/HintPill.tsx`): an outlined hint such as "Which weekend?" or "42 to review". Each tone has its own mark: a triangle for needs-you, a tick for done, the equaliser for busy, and none for info.
- `Ticket` (`src/components/ui/Ticket.tsx`): the true ticket stub (`docs/specs/ticket-stub.md`). `layout` is `columns` (body | stub end) or `rows` (phone), `size` is `hero` or `small`. Parts: `TicketKicker`, `TicketEventLine`, `TicketHeadline`, `TicketSmallLine`, `TicketFacts`, and stub atoms (`StubAdmit`, `StubShortDate`, `StubBarcode`, `StubKV`, `StubStamp`, `StubCode`, `StubSerial`). Stub atoms are hidden from screen readers; `StubSentence` gives the one plain sentence they read. Values come from `src/services/ticket.ts`; the component holds no rules. Notches are real holes (2026-11-26): the paper and border sit on a `::before` layer masked at the tear, placed from the stub's measured position, so whatever is behind the ticket shows through; the tear's dashes stop short of the holes.
- Landing look (`docs/specs/landing-look.md`, module `src/styles/landing-look.css`): `PosterHeading` (condensed poster face in capitals, optional `second` words in the second colour), `PostIt` (taped paper note, tones paper, second or accent; straightens and lifts on hover), `AppBackdrop` (the drifting accent-tinted contour texture behind the app) and `Logo` (the heartbeat logo, still under reduced motion). Tokens: `--font-poster`, `--la-second` and its soft and on-colour, one partner per accent. Ticket styles are their own module (`docs/specs/ticket-stub-styles.md`).
- `StubHeader` (`src/components/ui/StubHeader.tsx`): the framing stub for lists. A roomy title header with small dashed perforations between its segments, then one large perforation notched on the left and right above the rows; the notches are real holes, like the ticket's. The Table view does not use it.
- `NameList` and `NameSep` (`src/components/ui/NameList.tsx`): lineups and the acts you saw read like a lineup poster, names split by a small accent diamond instead of commas; screen readers hear a comma, and no line starts with a diamond. `joinNames` gives the same as plain text for titles and the share card. Used on the ticket headline and small line, Home's last show, Summary rows, the Table's acts, Fill gaps and the year review.
- `Stars` (`src/components/ui/Stars.tsx`): a rating as icon stars with a label ("Rated 4 of 5", or "Not rated" with faint outlines). With `onChange` it is a radio group: a tap on the current value clears it, and the chosen star pops once (skipped with reduced motion).
- `Equalizer` (`src/components/ui/Equalizer.tsx`): the busy mark, three bars that breathe while something is working, in place of a spinner. The words go beside it, and the bars stand still under reduced motion.
- `LineupTable` (`src/components/LineupTable.tsx`): the one place the lineup is drawn on the event detail. Renders lineup slots as a square-bordered `<table>` with a sticky mono-caps header (`Saw`, `Act`, `Billed as`, `Stage` hidden on phones, and `Set time`), a square `Tabs` day picker with counts, a `Segmented` stage filter (or a `<select>` when there are more than four stages), timed-only stages and set times, and the tick-to-add logic.
- `ReadRow` (`src/components/ui/ReadRow.tsx`, with `ReadList`): a quiet read row for a saved value people read more often than they change (shows icon, muted sans label, value or faint empty text, and optional source/inferred badge as siblings; tapping or pressing Enter swaps to the editor in place, and leaving or pressing Escape returns to read mode).
